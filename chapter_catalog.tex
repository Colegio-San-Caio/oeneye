\section{Operator physics}
Let $D^5$ be the 5-dimensional compact unit disk with metric $g_{ij}$ and potential $V$. The operator
\[
A = -\Delta_{D^5} + V(x), \qquad
\Delta_{D^5} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{|g|}} \sum_{i,j=1}^{5} \partial_i\!\left(\sqrt{|g|}\, g^{ij}\, \partial_j\right)
\]
is self-adjoint under Dirichlet or Neumann conditions on $\partial D^5 = S^4$.
